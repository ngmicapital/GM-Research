4 ⚖ Regulatory & Legal Radar

Thesis: the CLARITY Act is dead for this Congress, so the regulatory path of least resistance runs through agency rulemaking, not legislation, and nothing in the Senate's next votes changes that. Crypto is a legal-risk asset until a statute lands, and this week gives no statute.

Active Legislation

  • CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633): the House passed the bill 294–134 on July 17, 2025. The Senate failed to invoke cloture on the motion to proceed on Sept 15, 2026 by 49–50, short of the 60 votes needed.
  • Status: the bill is effectively shelved for the rest of this Congress. Democrats are projected to take both chambers in the next session, which lowers the odds of a lame-duck revival further.
  • Senate Agriculture track: the CFTC-side Digital Commodity Intermediaries Act is the other half of the market-structure package and is not on the floor.
  • GENIUS Act: the stablecoin framework enacted in 2025 remains the only federal crypto statute in force.

Today's Decision Points

  • Senate votes, 5:30pm ET Nov 16: the announced agenda omits the CLARITY Act, so there is no crypto floor event today.
  • House meeting, Nov 17: no crypto item identified in the sources I could reach.
  • SEC Regulation Crypto Assets: the SEC proposed it in August. If adopted, it would preempt state securities registration for exempt offerings, which makes the agency track the real near-term driver.
  • CFTC rules: expected to build on the March 2026 joint SEC and CFTC taxonomy. Leveraged trading on designated spot markets would fall under CFTC jurisdiction, though preemption is contested.
Regulatory risk is now a rulemaking and enforcement risk, not a bill risk. Without a statute, exchanges and brokers keep operating in a grey zone, which argues for smaller size on offshore venues and no position on regulatory-clarity headlines. The tail risk is an enforcement action rather than a vote.

  • NVDA fell about 12.75% in the week to Nov 13, extending the decline to six consecutive weekly losses, the longest such streak since 2012 per one account.
  • October record high was roughly $243, which frames the drawdown.
  • MAs: 50-day and 200-day moving averages were not fetched this run. EST
  • INTC: not covered in the sources I could reach.

Broader AI landscape

  • Anthropic said its Claude Mythos Preview took three days to port and optimise Claude for AMD's MI355 GPUs, with the model writing or porting all of the kernels.
  • Frontier-lab compute commitments are split: Nvidia about 39%, Broadcom custom chips 24%, Google TPUs 16%, AMD 15%, Amazon Trainium 6%, per a J.P. Morgan Asset Management chart.
  • Chip stocks lost more than $650B across two sessions after the Nov 5 rout, and the bond market repriced the Fed in the same window.
  • Taiwanese prosecutors have accused an Nvidia manager of helping smuggle B300-based servers into China, which the company disputes.
Why the CUDA moat matters: most AI software is written against Nvidia's CUDA programming layer, so switching chips has historically meant rewriting large amounts of code and tooling. If a frontier model can port and tune its own kernels for a rival GPU in days, the switching cost falls, and the market starts pricing a multi-vendor future. That is a margin story as much as a volume story, because price-setting power depends on lock-in, not just on who ships the most chips.
The AI capex cycle is still broadening in spending but narrowing in pricing power. Hold a small AMD long as the cleaner expression of the porting news and keep NVDA size small until guidance answers the moat question. A strong guide with rising supply-constrained pricing would change my view within a session.

7 🌎 Geopolitical Calendar

Thesis: Hormuz and the Iran file are the geopolitical transmission channel into the Fed path. A headline that lifts Brent is a yield story before it is an oil story, and I'd treat oil above $95 as a risk-off signal for crypto.

🏴 Iran war and Hormuz shipping (Active)
Status: President Trump has said ending the Iran war does not require a deal, and Brent climbed back toward $96 after the Iran-deal rejection. The IRGC claimed a mine strike on a gas carrier in Hormuz on Nov 13, which the sources I could reach do not independently confirm.
Transmission Mechanism: Oil above $95 feeds headline inflation, which keeps December hike odds high and yields elevated. That hits long-duration tech and bitcoin through real yields, and it does so faster than it hits the energy complex.
Reversal Signal: A framework deal that holds through escorted tanker transits, with Brent back below $90, would remove the inflation impulse and give the Fed room to slow.
🏴 Houthi strikes and Saudi pumping stations (Active)
Status: Houthi attacks on Saudi pumping stations and continued Russia-Ukraine strikes on energy assets keep supply risk alive. Analysts expect Brent to test $90–92 next week while noting that geopolitical risk is still capping the downside.
Transmission Mechanism: Supply disruptions on the Saudi overland route raise freight and delivered-cost premiums, which push up the cost of imported energy for Asia and feed the same inflation loop as the Iran file.
Reversal Signal: Uninterrupted flows through Saudi overland bypasses and a sustained drop in the Oman-to-Dubai premium would signal the supply shock is fading.
🏴 US–China chip export enforcement (Active)
Status: Taiwanese prosecutors have indicted a manager over alleged B300 server resales to China, and Senators Warren and Coons have written about Nvidia-chip purchases by a third party. Nvidia has disputed the allegations and the case is not proven.
Transmission Mechanism: Enforcement risk becomes a revenue-share and guidance risk for Nvidia, which hits the AI-chip complex and the sector rotation in section 2 directly. It is an equity-led channel rather than a macro one, but it is landing in the same week as the rates shock.
Reversal Signal: A clean board response to the regulatory questions inside the window reported for it, plus a guide that does not depend on China shipments, would stop the bleeding.
Geopolitics matters today only through oil and Nvidia. If Brent holds above $95 while Nvidia guidance softens, the combined effect is a hawkish Fed and a weaker AI tape, and both land on bitcoin. The clean de-risking trigger is a Brent close below $90 together with a supportive Nvidia print.
