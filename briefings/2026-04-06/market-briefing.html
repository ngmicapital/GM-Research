⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar
The most important regulatory development of the past month — and arguably the most important for US crypto markets since the spot ETF approvals — is the SEC-CFTC joint digital commodity classification framework (March 17, 2026). The 68-page interpretive document jointly signed by SEC Chair Atkins and CFTC Chair Selig officially designates 16 crypto assets as "digital commodities": BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, ADA, AVAX, LINK, DOT, HBAR, LTC, BCH, SHIB, XLM, XTZ, APT. The "digital commodity" designation means these assets are explicitly not securities, removing SEC enforcement exposure for the exchanges listing them and eliminating the threat of unregistered securities actions. The MOU between SEC and CFTC (March 11) formalised coordination. This is regulatory clarity traders have waited years for — it's a structural bid under the entire asset class.
