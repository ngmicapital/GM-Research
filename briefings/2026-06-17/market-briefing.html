4 ⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar EST

The GENIUS Act's implementation clock is running — the stablecoin framework passed in July 2025 and Treasury's AML/CTF rulemaking (NPRM filed April 2026) now sets the conditions for which issuers survive. The act officially takes effect January 2027, or 120 days after final rules — whichever comes first. With most final rules required within one year of the statute's effective date, the regulatory window is narrowing. The operative question for crypto traders: which stablecoins get licensed, and which get driven offshore?

Active Legislation / Rulemaking

GENIUS Act (S.1582): Effective Jan 18, 2027 or 120 days post-final-rules. Treasury NPRM on AML/CTF now in public comment phase. OCC bulletins being issued on bank custody of stablecoins

FDIC / OCC: Bank custody rules for crypto being finalized; critical for ETF custodians and institutional on-ramps

EU MiCA (live): European stablecoin issuers already operating under MiCA requirements; USDT's EUR-denominated market share being contested by MiCA-compliant alternatives

Today's Decision Points

FOMC statement language: If Warsh removes language about "monitoring inflation" in favor of "ensuring price stability," interpret as a genuine hawk — stablecoins and DeFi see more regulatory clarity risk under a harder-money regime

GENIUS Act gaps: Bank Policy Institute (June 13) flagged four flaws: operational/illicit finance risk inadequately addressed; redemption mechanics can trigger destabilizing runs; stablecoin holder rights unclear; state vs. federal overlap unresolved

EU Viva Tech (Jun 17–20): France and Germany expected to announce a formal definition of "sovereign technology" based on HQ location, data residency, R&D presence — material for EU crypto node operators and cloud providers

USDC and USDT both face compliance overhead under GENIUS Act AML/CTF rules. Smaller algorithmic stablecoins face an existential risk. The January 2027 deadline is close enough to be priced — watch for announcements from Circle (USDC) and Tether on their compliance roadmaps in Q3 2026.
