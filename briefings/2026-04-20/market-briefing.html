📚 Learn Something: CoWoS Advanced Packaging
The real bottleneck in AI chips isn't the silicon — it's how you package it. CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) is TSMC's advanced packaging technology that stacks multiple chiplets (GPU die, HBM memory) onto a single interposer. Think of it as the "foundation" that lets AI chips talk to their memory at blazing speed. Why does this matter? NVIDIA's Blackwell and Rubin GPUs require CoWoS to achieve their performance targets, but TSMC's CoWoS capacity is the tightest constraint in the supply chain. TSMC has been expanding CoWoS capacity aggressively — doubling it through 2025-2026 — but demand still outstrips supply. This is why "Blackwell sold out through mid-2026" isn't a sales pitch, it's a packaging bottleneck reality. For investors, CoWoS capacity expansion announcements from TSMC are as important as the chip designs themselves.
NVDA's April rally is deserved on fundamentals ($62.3B quarterly data center revenue). The Cursor $50B raise validates AI software as its own investable category, not just an NVDA derivative. The risk remains Rubin delays — if the memory issue pushes the timeline, the 21% April run gives back fast.
