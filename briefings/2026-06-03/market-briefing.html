The US crypto regulatory pipeline is the most constructive in years — GENIUS Act implementation regs are due July 18 (stablecoin framework now law), and the Clarity Act (digital asset market structure) is advancing through Senate Banking Committee — but the near-term price action is disconnecting from fundamental regulatory progress.

📑 Active Legislation

GENIUS Act (Law — signed July 2025): Stablecoin regulation enacted. Requires 1:1 reserves (cash/T-bills), monthly disclosure. Critical date: July 18, 2026 — OCC, Fed, FDIC implementation regulations due. Stablecoin yield prohibition is the contested provision; crypto industry pushing back in Senate Banking hearings.

Clarity Act (Senate Banking): Digital asset market structure bill. Unveiled May 11, 2026 by Senate Banking Committee. Attempts to resolve the SEC vs. CFTC jurisdiction battle over crypto. Hearing underway — bipartisan support but carve-out negotiations ongoing.

Fed Funds Rate: 3.50–3.75% (unchanged for third consecutive meeting, Apr 2026 FOMC). New Chair Kevin Warsh (replaced Powell, May 15, 2026). June 16–17 FOMC: 98% probability No Change per Polymarket.

Services PMI (today): Manufacturing has already softened. If Services PMI prints below 50, the stagflation calculus changes — the Fed would face slowing growth + elevated energy-driven inflation simultaneously.
