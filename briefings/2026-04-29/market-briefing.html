5 🤖 AI & Semiconductor Watch

The OpenAI revenue miss is a demand-signal scare, not a supply-side problem — and that distinction matters. NVDA, AMD, TSMC all sold off 3–5% on the WSJ report that OpenAI missed internal revenue and user targets, with CFO Friar warning that compute contracts could be harder to fund. But the key nuance: OpenAI's problem is monetization, not compute demand. NVDA's $78B Q1 guidance is hardware orders already placed. The real question is whether the next $100B of orders (2027+) materializes if AI applications don't generate sufficient revenue. For now, the capex cycle is intact — but the end-demand validation that justifies $5T NVDA is being questioned for the first time.
