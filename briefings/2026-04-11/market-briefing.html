Active Legislation

GENIUS Act (S.1582): Signed into law July 18, 2025. Treasury FinCEN + OFAC just proposed implementing rules for AML/sanctions requirements on stablecoin issuers. Takes effect Jan 18, 2027 (or 120 days post-final rules).

Reg Crypto Framework: SEC "Project Crypto" — rulemaking for crypto exchange trading, tokenized securities guidance, and innovation exemption in progress throughout 2026.
