Path of least resistance for rates: hold, monitor, and watch Hormuz reopening timing.

⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar
The most important regulatory development of the week: SEC Reg Crypto sent to OIRA. The SEC's two-tier safe harbor framework (submitted April 6) proposes startups raise up to $5M over four years, established projects up to $75M annually, both with streamlined disclosure. Combined with the March 17 SEC-CFTC joint guidance classifying BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP as digital commodities under CFTC jurisdiction, the US regulatory overhang is materially clearing. XRP's +5.5% Friday move is the market pricing this in. Key risk: Senate Banking Committee markup targeted late April could introduce weakening amendments.
