📚 Learn Something: Dark Pools
Dark pools are private trading venues where institutions execute large block orders without showing their hand to the public market. When a fund wants to buy $2B of NVDA, placing that on a lit exchange would spike the price before the order fills. Instead, they route through dark pools where orders match anonymously. The tell: when dark pool volume surges (like NVDA's $2.69B print on April 10) while price is flat, it signals stealth accumulation — large buyers positioning without tipping off the tape. You can track dark pool prints via FINRA's ATS transparency data, published with a ~2-week lag.
The packaging play is the new edge. NVDA's TSMC lock-up creates a structural moat beyond chip architecture — stay long NVDA and MU into the Rubin cycle.
