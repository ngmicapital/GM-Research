Senator Moreno stated plainly that if the bill doesn't pass by month-end, digital asset legislation is off the table for the foreseeable future. The yield compromise — banning stablecoin yield-on-reserves while allowing activity-based rewards — cleared a major hurdle. Polymarket odds for passage jumped above 60%. This is the structural catalyst that could rerate the entire crypto sector if it passes.

7 🌐 Geopolitical Calendar
🚨 US-Iran Negotiations — ACTIVE
Status: A 14-point memorandum of understanding is reported near completion. Trump paused Project Freedom in the Strait of Hormuz. However, Iran's Mohsen Rezaei demands reparations, and the US naval blockade remains in place.
Transmission Mechanism: Hormuz handles ~20M barrels/day (20% of global seaborne oil). A deal sends WTI sub-$90 and unlocks a risk-on move across equities and crypto. Failure means WTI retests $110+, stagflation returns, and the Fed is forced into hawkishness.
Reversal: Iran publicly accepts the MOU terms, US lifts sanctions, and Hormuz shipping normalizes. Watch for Iranian Foreign Minister statements and US State Department press conferences.
🚨 US-China Tariff Regime — HOLDING
Status: November 2025 deal extended tariff reduction through November 2026. Current effective rate at 10% on most goods after Supreme Court struck down 2025 tariff basis. Kalshi: 68% odds tariff stays at 10–20% on July 1.
Transmission Mechanism: Tariff stability supports global supply chains and reduces input costs for US manufacturers. Re-escalation (probability ~15%) would spike DXY, compress margins for import-dependent sectors, and trigger risk-off in emerging markets and crypto.
Reversal: Watch for executive orders post-November 2026, or any breakdown in diplomatic channels. China's rare earth export controls remain a lever.
🚨 US Government Shutdown — 50+ Days
Status: DHS partial shutdown ended April 30, but broader fiscal uncertainty remains. Kalshi's $22.7M in volume signals this is the highest-conviction political trade on the platform. FY2026 appropriations remain incomplete.
Transmission Mechanism: Extended shutdowns delay federal data releases (jobs, CPI), reduce government spending velocity, and create headline risk. Markets have largely ignored this shutdown, but a full-government shutdown would be different.
Reversal: Full FY2026 appropriations package. No deadline set.
Iran is the swing factor for every macro market right now. The deal framework exists but isn't signed. Oil's 10% weekly decline already prices significant deal probability — meaning the downside from a failed deal is asymmetrically large. China tariffs are a known quantity until November. The government shutdown is priced as noise.
