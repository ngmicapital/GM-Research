4 ⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar

The most important regulatory development this week is the 100+ crypto firm coalition pressing the Senate Banking Committee to mark up the CLARITY Act. This is the market-structure bill that creates a federal framework for digital assets, finally resolving the SEC-CFTC jurisdiction question. The SEC-CFTC joint MOU from March already established a five-category token taxonomy, and the SEC's April 13 DeFi interface relief shows the regulatory posture has fundamentally shifted from "enforcement-first" to "rulemaking mode" under Atkins. The GENIUS Act stablecoin rules are being implemented by both Treasury and FDIC.

📜 Active Legislation
  • CLARITY Act — Senate markup urged by 100+ firms (Coinbase, Ripple, Circle). Banking Committee target May. Polymarket prices passage at 48%.
  • GENIUS Act (Stablecoins) — FDIC + Treasury implementing now. Payment stablecoin issuer rulemaking published.
  • SEC-CFTC MOU (Mar 17) — 68-page joint guidance, 5-category taxonomy. Binding framework supersedes 2019.
  • XRP commodity classification — SEC + CFTC both confirmed. Spot XRP ETFs hold $1.53B AUM.
