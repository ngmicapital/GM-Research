What a reversal looks like: Iran back-channels hardliner statement rejecting Strait compliance; OR Trump restarts airstrikes language; OR a new incident at Kharg Island. Any of these triggers an oil +8-12% repricing in pre-market.

What a reversal looks like: House vote passes DHS funding — DXY +0.3-0.5%, modest bond rally. No resolution today means Week 8+ and growing political costs for House Republicans heading to midterms.
🟡 CLARITY ACT SENATE TIMELINE — APRIL 13-30 WINDOW IS EVERYTHING
Transmission mechanism: The CLARITY Act defines whether BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, and the broader altcoin ecosystem operate under securities or commodities law. If the Banking Committee advances the bill in April, it re-rates every asset with regulatory uncertainty — primarily ETH (commodity status would unlock ETH staking ETF products), SOL, and XRP. If it fails, the SEC continues its Reg Crypto exemption alternative path — which is narrower and slower, but keeps the regulatory ambiguity premium in altcoin prices.
What a reversal looks like: Senate Banking Committee advances the bill after April 13 return → ETH and altcoin-heavy portfolios reprice +8-15% on the week; XRP most direct beneficiary.
🟡 US-CHINA TARIFF STRUCTURE — JULY 1 RESET APPROACHING
Transmission mechanism: Kalshi pricing 69% odds of 10-19.99% tariff rate on China by July 1 — moderate regime, not escalation. But the tariff uncertainty is suppressing supply chain investment and keeping manufacturing PMIs soft globally. A tariff reduction would be a direct positive for consumer goods inflation and could be a Fed-easing catalyst in H2 2026. Conversely, tariff escalation above 20% feeds directly into CPI through imported goods.
What a reversal looks like: Tariff rate set at 20%+ → CPI upside risk, further delay in Fed easing, consumer discretionary and retail stocks re-rate lower.
