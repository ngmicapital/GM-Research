⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar

The most important regulatory development this week is the SEC-CFTC Interpretive Release (March 17, 2026) — the first joint statement clarifying how federal securities laws apply to airdrops, protocol mining, protocol staking, and non-security asset wrapping. This is consequential: it redraws the jurisdictional boundary between what the SEC owns and what the CFTC owns, directly impacting how exchanges structure listings and custody. The GENIUS Act stablecoin implementation is the next major industry catalyst — issuers must choose federal vs. state licensing pathways now to meet the mid-2026 rule finalisation.
