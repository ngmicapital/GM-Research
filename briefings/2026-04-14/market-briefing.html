⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar
The SEC-CFTC harmonisation is the most important crypto regulatory development of the cycle. On March 17, 2026, both agencies issued a comprehensive joint interpretation under a Memorandum of Understanding — declaring most digital assets non-securities and establishing a taxonomy: digital commodities, digital collectibles, digital tools, stablecoins, and digital securities. This bypasses years of litigation and provides the institutional clarity that has been blocking corporate treasury and fund adoption. The shift in tone from enforcement to "minimum effective dose" regulation is a structural bull catalyst for the industry.

CFTC MOU with SEC (signed March 11, 2026): Coordinated oversight. CFTC retains jurisdiction over digital commodities (BTC, ETH).
