⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar
The most important regulatory development of the year arrived on March 17: the SEC-CFTC joint interpretive release classifying 16 crypto assets as digital commodities. This is not formal rulemaking — it is an interpretive release — but it will govern enforcement posture for the remainder of 2026. Placing BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, and 12 others explicitly under CFTC jurisdiction removes the primary legal uncertainty that has blocked institutional allocations. The capital-allocation response from pension funds and endowments takes 1–3 quarters to execute — the March ETF inflow rebound (+$1.32B) is early evidence of re-entry beginning. The SEC essentially conceded the jurisdictional argument it has been making since 2021. This is bullish for every asset on the list.
