⚖️ 4. Regulatory & Legal Radar

The most important regulatory development of the week is the SEC-CFTC coordination framework coming into full implementation. The March 17 joint interpretation — backed by the March 11 MOU — creates a coherent token taxonomy for the first time: digital commodities, collectibles, tools, stablecoins, and digital securities each get a defined regime. This is the most significant US regulatory clarity event in crypto history, and its implications for institutional onboarding have not yet been fully priced.

Active Legislation

CLARITY Act — Market structure bill. Senate hearings restarted Jan 2026. White House crypto czar David Sacks says "closer to passage than any prior point." Settles SEC vs. CFTC jurisdictional split. Status: Committee stage.

GENIUS Act — Stablecoin regulation. Enacted 2025. OCC licensing/custody requirements being finalized; implementation deadline mid-2026. Permitted stablecoins are NOT securities/commodities — separate regime.

SEC-CFTC MOU — Signed March 11, 2026. Token taxonomy issued March 17. Now operational.
