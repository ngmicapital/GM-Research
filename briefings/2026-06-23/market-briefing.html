7 🌐 Geopolitical Calendar

The Iran-Hormuz situation is in a delicate de-escalation window — early normalisation signs are pulling WTI lower, but Polymarket prices only a 2.8% chance of any Iran uranium enrichment deal by June 30. The oil market is pricing de-escalation; the prediction market is pricing continued conflict. One of them will be wrong.

⚠ Iran / Strait of Hormuz
Status: IEA characterises the 2026 Iran war as "the largest supply disruption in the history of the global oil market." However: ADNOC (Abu Dhabi) and Kuwait are resuming supply; Iran is cutting prices on cargoes to China; Hormuz passage showing early normalisation. WTI has fallen from ~$88 to ~$74/bbl over the past week.
Transmission Mechanism: WTI drives inflation expectations → Fed policy → USD → BTC. A further WTI decline to $70/bbl would reduce CPI pressure and marginally improve the odds of a late-2026 Fed cut, which is the single biggest macro tailwind for crypto.
Reversal: Any Hormuz strait closure announcement, Iranian naval action, or US military escalation would reverse WTI sharply higher and re-activate the stagflation scenario. Polymarket: Iran enrichment deal June 30 at 2.8% — conflict is the base case.
⚠ US–Iran Diplomacy Stalemate
Status: Diplomats active on a framework covering uranium enrichment, sanctions, and regional security. June 2: Iran's Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei declared US military bases in the Middle East "no longer safe." Both sides are talking but rhetoric is hardening.
Transmission Mechanism: A deal removes the oil-shock premium and releases ~2–3M bbl/day of Iranian supply, pushing WTI to $65–70/bbl range. That's a net deflationary shock that directly opens the door for Fed cuts — crypto's most direct macro catalyst.
Reversal: Breakdown in talks (Polymarket: 97.2% base case) keeps the oil premium elevated and FOMC on hold.
⚠ Trump Administration — Domestic Policy Overhang
Status: PredictIt: 46% chance Trump leaves office early (this is PredictIt noise, not signal — this contract has been elevated for months). RCP job approval 40.2–40.8% by June 24. Anti-Weaponization fund payouts: 3% chance this year.
Transmission Mechanism: Political uncertainty creates episodic risk-off spikes. GENIUS Act and CLARITY Act are Trump-administration-friendly crypto regulation — political instability could delay signature or veto window.
Reversal: Stable approval trajectory and continued crypto-friendly executive posture is the base case; watch Truth Social for deal announcements or executive orders.
⚠ Japan Economic Stress
Status: Nikkei +1.55% today (yen weakness tailwind). But Polymarket: Japan recession in 2026 at 14.5%. BOJ policy normalization vs. fiscal stress is the underlying tension. Japan holds significant US Treasury positions — any JGB (Japanese Government Bond) yield volatility that forces Japanese institutional selling of US Treasuries could spike the 10Y further.
Transmission Mechanism: BOJ → US Treasuries → US 10Y yield → USD → crypto. The Japanese bond market is the "financial system's sensitivity meter."
Reversal: BOJ confirms continued policy hold; Japanese institutions stay in US Treasuries. Yen stability below 155/USD is the tell.
