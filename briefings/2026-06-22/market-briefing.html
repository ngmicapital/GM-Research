🔒 What Is Intel 18A? Intel's most advanced chip manufacturing process — "18A" refers to an Angstrom-scale node comparable to TSMC's N2. Intel ceded its manufacturing lead to TSMC through the 2010s–2020s; the 18A foundry program is the attempt to reclaim it. Apple, Google, and NVIDIA simultaneously designing or evaluating chips on this process is the first credible signal the bet is working. At commercial volume ramp, this shifts chip geopolitics meaningfully: TSMC remains a Taiwan-based concentration risk, and a US domestic advanced node foundry reduces that dependency.
AI capex is in a broadening phase, not saturation. The INTC/MRVL/NVDA complex is the structural long. Risk: if Intel 18A yield rates disappoint at volume in H2 2026, the commercial validation narrative unwinds quickly.
