4 ⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar EST

The CLARITY Act is advancing through Senate Banking Committee with stablecoin yield as the final live negotiating point — the Tillis-Alsobrooks compromise that landed in May is net positive for the crypto industry and likely to pass committee before summer recess. The compromise bars yield that is "economically or functionally equivalent to a bank deposit" but explicitly permits "bona fide activities" — meaning Coinbase can continue paying USDC rewards to users while Circle cannot pay direct yield on USDC issuance. This is workable for the industry; Coinbase and Circle both backed the deal immediately. Meanwhile, Treasury published a proposed rule under the already-enacted GENIUS Act targeting illicit finance flows through stablecoin issuers.

Active Legislation
  • CLARITY Act — Senate Banking Committee stage. Stablecoin yield compromise finalised (Tillis-Alsobrooks). Industry-backed. Likely committee vote before July recess. Direct catalyst for COIN, USDC-adjacent protocols, and US-regulated crypto exchanges.
  • GENIUS Act — Already law (signed 2025). Treasury proposed implementation rule for illicit finance AML requirements on stablecoin issuers. 60-day comment period open. Coinbase/Circle/Paxos expected to file substantive comments.
  • Section 122 Tariff Ruling — US Court of International Trade found 10% global tariffs unlawful on May 7. Government appealing to Federal Circuit. If upheld on appeal: consumer goods deflation shock. If reversed: inflationary floor remains.
Decision Points This Week
  • CLARITY Act yield language — Final text being reviewed by crypto lawyers. Any last-minute amendment re-opening the "bona fide activities" carveout could delay committee vote. Watch CoinDesk policy coverage.
  • Treasury GENIUS rule comments — Public comment period open. Outcome of comments shapes the definition of "stablecoin issuer" — whether it captures DeFi protocols is the live question.
  • BTC spot ETF options expansion — CBOE expanding crypto options product suite for ETF products. Increased derivatives liquidity on ETF products should reduce spot vol over time — positive structural development.
  • Section 122 tariff appeal track — Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit (CAFC) docket. Most significant non-crypto legal development with direct macro implications.
CLARITY Act passage is the single largest structural catalyst remaining for US crypto markets. Timeline estimate: Senate floor vote H2 2026, conference + White House signature Q4 2026.
