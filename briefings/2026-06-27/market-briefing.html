Active Legislation
  • GENIUS Act — Signed July 2025. Final rules deadline: July 18, 2026. Covers issuer licensing, capital requirements, custody standards, AML provisions.
  • Stablecoin yield battle — Banks pressing regulators to close the "yield loophole." Outcome reshapes competitive dynamics between TradFi and DeFi stablecoin products.
  • FDIC bank subsidiary stablecoins — FDIC proposed procedures for bank subsidiaries to issue stablecoins; signals growing TradFi on-ramp intent.
  • Global regulatory convergence — 7 major economies (US, EU, UK, Singapore, HK, UAE, Japan) now mandate full reserve backing, licensed issuers, guaranteed redemption rights.
