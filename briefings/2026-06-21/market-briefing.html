4 ⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar

The CLARITY Act is now on the Senate Legislative Calendar — the crypto market structure bill advanced by the Banking Committee 15-9 on May 14, placing a floor vote within reach before the August recess. This is the most concrete legislative progress since the GENIUS Act (stablecoin) was signed into law earlier this year.

Active Legislation
  • CLARITY Act — On Senate Legislative Calendar under General Orders. 309-page compromise text includes DeFi trading protocol framework, insolvency safe harbor for digital commodity transactions, and strengthened AML provisions. Two Democrats voted yes in committee; floor vote requires 60-vote threshold — watch for bipartisan deals.
  • GENIUS Act (stablecoin) — Already law. Banking groups flag a “yield loophole” allowing activity-based rewards on stablecoins while prohibiting outright interest payments — implementation will be contentious.
  • Crypto Ethics Provision — Unresolved issue around government officials’ ties to crypto industry is a sticking point that could delay the CLARITY floor vote; watch for amendment proposals next week.
Today’s Decision Points
  • Senate floor timeline — CLARITY on the Legislative Calendar but midterms and shutdown risk in H2 2026 compress the window; August recess effectively ends meaningful floor time. If not passed by July, the bill likely dies until the new Congress.
  • GENIUS implementation — Stablecoin issuers navigating the yield/reward distinction. Circle, Tether, and bank-affiliated issuers will each interpret “activity-based rewards” differently; expect rulemaking conflicts through Q3.
  • DeFi Protocol Oversight — CLARITY’s DeFi framework is the first statutory attempt to define trading protocol obligations. Uniswap, dYdX, and GMX are each monitoring; ambiguity on what constitutes a “trading protocol” under the bill remains.
Regulatory thesis: The CLARITY Act reaching the Senate floor calendar is the most bullish structural development for crypto in 2026 — regulatory certainty has historically been a BTC catalyst within 3–6 months of passage. But the 60-vote threshold in a divided Senate and the ethics provision deadlock make a summer passage anything but guaranteed. Assign ~35% probability to passage before August recess.

🧠 Foundry 101: “Foundry” in semiconductors means manufacturing chips designed by someone else (like Apple) using your own fabrication plants (fabs). TSMC is the dominant foundry globally; Intel Foundry Services (IFS) is Intel’s attempt to compete. Apple designing its own chips (Apple Silicon — M-series, A-series) but manufacturing at a rival to TSMC is a monumental industrial policy achievement. It locks Intel into a predictable revenue stream independent of its own chip sales — which is why the market re-rated INTC so aggressively (+10.6% on the announcement).
