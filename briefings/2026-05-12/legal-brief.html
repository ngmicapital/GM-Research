ABA CEO Rob Nichols published a "contact your senator" appeal to member bankers ahead of Thursday's executive session; a parallel ABA-plus-state-bankers letter to the OCC opens a second front against alleged "yield loopholes" in the OCC's GENIUS Act NPRM (Bulletin 2026-3, comment window closed 1 May). Sens. Thom Tillis (R-NC) and Angela Alsobrooks (D-MD), whose 2 May Section 404 compromise text the coalition rejected, have not budged. Their public position remains that the deal — banning yield "economically or functionally equivalent" to bank deposits while preserving rewards tied to "bona fide activities" (payments, trading, transfers) — is final. Banking trades counter with research-cited claims that yield-bearing-stablecoin deposit flight "could reduce consumer, small-business, and agricultural lending by one-fifth or more."

The markup itself remains noticed for Thursday 14 May 10:30 AM ET in Dirksen 538 (Senate Banking Committee press notice via Chair Tim Scott). 📎 Historical precedent: Glass-Steagall's 1999 Gramm-Leach-Bliley repeal followed an analogous trade-association mobilisation pattern — eleventh-hour escalation when committee text was already locked. Pattern recognition: industry-coalition mobilisation rarely reverses chairman-blessed compromise text post-notice.

Chair Tim Scott and the Tillis-Alsobrooks deal-holders need Manchin/Fetterman-style Democrats; the public coalition pressure helps Republicans hold the centre. Counsel for stablecoin issuers (Circle, PayPal, Ripple RLUSD, USDC distributors) should pre-position interpretive memos on what "bona fide activities" means — that's the next litigation surface.

The 6 May transaction: Ripple redeemed a portion of its OUSG (Ondo Short-Term US Government Treasuries) holdings on the XRP Ledger. Ondo processed the redemption and initiated a fiat payout instruction via Mastercard's Multi-Token Network (MTN), which routed to Kinexys by JP Morgan. Kinexys debited Ondo's Blockchain Deposit Account and settled USD proceeds to Ripple's correspondent bank account in Singapore. End-to-end settle time: under five seconds; settled outside traditional banking hours.

The legal-structural significance is the integration model — four separate infrastructure layers operating atomically without legacy CLS or SWIFT intermediation. JP Morgan's blockchain-deposit-account product (the on-ledger commercial bank money rail that survived Warsh-style narrow-bank scrutiny — see Story #2) plays the legal-tender pricing function. Mastercard's MTN plays the routing function. XRPL plays the underlying-asset transfer function. Ondo's OUSG fund (a Delaware limited partnership investing through BlackRock's BUIDL into US Treasuries) plays the asset-tokenisation function.

📎 Historical precedent: BIS Project Agorá and Project Mariana atomic-FX experiments (2024-25) established the technical baseline; this is the first commercial production cross-border atomic settle outside a central-bank-coordinated sandbox.
