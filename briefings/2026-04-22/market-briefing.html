5 ⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar
SEC's "Regulation Crypto" is at the White House doorstep — the most consequential US crypto rulemaking in a decade is weeks away from publication. SEC Chair Paul Atkins confirmed the proposal has been submitted to OIRA (Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs) — the final step before publication. The framework creates three new exemptions: a 4-year startup exemption (up to $5M raises), a fundraising exemption (up to $75M in 12 months), and an investment contract safe harbor for tokens that achieve decentralisation. OIRA reviews typically take 30-90 days, but political pressure suggests an accelerated timeline.
Regulation Crypto: Three Pillars

1. Startup exemption: Time-limited (up to 4 years) for investment contract offerings involving crypto assets, with a cap of $5M in fundraising. Designed for early-stage protocols.

2. Fundraising exemption: Allows issuers to raise up to $75M during a 12-month period with lighter disclosure requirements than traditional securities offerings.

3. Safe harbor for decentralised tokens: Once a project can demonstrate sufficient decentralisation, the token no longer qualifies as a security. This is the nuclear option — it creates a clear off-ramp from securities law for mature protocols.

📚 Learn Something: CoWoS Advanced Packaging
CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) is the advanced packaging technology that TSMC uses to connect multiple chiplets into a single high-performance module. Think of it as the "glue" that holds modern AI chips together. NVIDIA's Blackwell and Rubin GPUs don't work as monolithic dies — they're composed of multiple smaller chiplets that need to communicate at extremely high bandwidth with minimal latency. CoWoS provides that interconnect layer. The reason it matters for investors: CoWoS capacity was the single biggest bottleneck for Blackwell production in 2025. TSMC has been aggressively expanding CoWoS capacity, investing an estimated $5-8B in new packaging lines. When NVIDIA says "Rubin is in full production," what they really mean is "TSMC has enough CoWoS capacity to package Rubin at scale." The bottleneck has shifted from silicon fabrication to advanced packaging — and TSMC is the only company on Earth that can do it at the required volume.
Amazon's $25B Anthropic deal confirms the AI capex supercycle is accelerating, not plateauing. But the custom chip exploration trend (Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, Google, Amazon) means NVDA's revenue growth continues while its market share at the margin shrinks. The trade is: own NVDA for the absolute demand growth, but hedge with AVGO and MRVL for the custom silicon diversification wave.
