Morning Edge · Sydney · 25 June 2026

Macro calm spread, but BTC still cannot reclaim value

Conviction: medium-high · Read time: 8 minutes

TL;DR

Oil kept unwinding and equity vol stayed soft, but BTC still spent the session stuck around $60.9k and below higher-timeframe value. Until crypto can reclaim weekly value near $62.28k and then push through $63.23k, macro relief is background noise rather than a clean buy signal.

The tactical map is simpler than the narrative. Energy stress is easing, which helps the broad tape, but crypto still prints seller-led orderflow, a bearish EMA cascade, and negative basis. That combination argues for patience, not anticipation.

1. Global Macro Snapshot

Macro view: the fear impulse kept fading, but it still has not translated into a genuine risk-on signal for crypto.

Yesterday's thesis was that oil unwinding should help the macro tape faster than it helps crypto. That continued to play out on 25 June 2026: crude extended its reversal as Hormuz traffic normalized, equity vol stayed contained, and the dollar did not add a fresh tightening shock.

The missing piece is confirmation from higher-beta assets. If the system were truly rotating back into risk, crypto should be stabilizing first or at least keeping up. It is not.

2. Equities

Equities view: lower oil is still supportive, but the better trade remains selective quality over blind beta.

The main bullish input for equities is that the market no longer has to discount an immediate supply shock in energy. That matters for inflation expectations and for any sector whose multiples had started to compress on stagflation anxiety.

But a calmer oil tape is not the same thing as a new growth impulse. If the market keeps bouncing, the cleaner expression still looks like quality, cashflow, and infrastructure rather than a wholesale chase into the weakest cyclicals.

3. Crypto

Crypto view: BTC is trading below value, below weekly VWAP, and still under a bearish higher-timeframe structure.

Today's Porygon run is unusually clear. BTC is sitting almost exactly on a dense daily confluence around $60,995, but the bigger picture is still weak: weekly VWAP and prior weekly low cluster at $62,282.90, prior daily high plus fib resistance sits at $63,227.54, and price remains in a bearish 4h structure with sellers still controlling delta.

There is one nuance bulls can use: funding is slightly negative and the book is bid-heavy, so sharp squeezes are possible. But unless those squeezes reclaim and hold $62.28k, they are more likely to be inventory clearing than regime change.

4. Regulatory

Regulatory view: policy is not the day's lead driver, but it is still capping how quickly speculative capital comes back.

The regulatory calendar still points toward implementation and perimeter-setting rather than a single fresh shock. That is enough to stop policy from dominating today's tape, but not enough to remove it as a medium-term drag on crypto beta.

Translation: traders can treat regulation as second-order for today's session, but not as something that has actually gone away.

5. AI & Semis

AI & semis view: deployable infrastructure still has the cleaner narrative than application-layer hype.

NVIDIA's AgentPerf messaging matters because it reframes the AI debate around agent throughput, concurrency, and power efficiency rather than abstract frontier prestige. That keeps the advantage with infrastructure names that make enterprise deployment easier and cheaper.

If software or application names rally, they can still move hard. But the strategic leadership remains closer to compute, networking, and the stack that turns agent demos into production systems.

6. Prediction Markets

Prediction-market read: geopolitical tail risk cooled, but it has not disappeared from the narrative stack.

Prediction markets linked to Hormuz traffic and broader Middle East escalation are no longer pricing the same panic they were earlier this month, which fits the oil unwind. But they also are not pricing a clean all-clear; they still imply that traders expect more headline volatility before the region is fully normal again.

That matters because these markets are often the fastest read on whether "macro calm" is real or just temporary. For now, they support relief, not complacency.

7. Geopolitical Risk

Geo view: the regime has shifted from panic to uneasy normalization.

More tanker traffic through Hormuz and softer crude tell you the supply shock channel is fading. That is the correct market response and the main reason broad risk has room to breathe.

But fading panic is not the same as restored stability. Until the market stops reacting sharply to each regional headline, geopolitical risk should be treated as dormant volatility rather than resolved volatility.

8. Today's Watchlist

  1. BTC $60,995. This is the immediate daily battle line. Lose it cleanly and the long-liquidation pocket below becomes easier to reach.
  2. BTC $62,282.90. Reclaiming weekly VWAP and prior weekly low is the first real test of whether bulls can repair structure.
  3. BTC $63,227.54. If price reaches prior daily high plus fib resistance, watch whether sellers reappear aggressively.
  4. WTI follow-through. More downside in crude keeps the inflation-scare unwind alive and should help the macro backdrop.
  5. VIX under 18. If vol stays calm while crypto remains weak, that divergence stays bearish for crypto bulls.
