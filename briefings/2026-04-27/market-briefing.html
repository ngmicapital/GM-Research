7. 🌐 Geopolitical Calendar
Strait of Hormuz Dual Blockade
Status: Effectively closed since Feb 28. US blockades Iranian ports; IRGC blocks strait passage, lays sea mines, boards merchant vessels. Ceasefire extended indefinitely on April 22 but no shipping has resumed.
Transmission mechanism: 25% of global seaborne oil + 20% of LNG transited Hormuz pre-conflict. IEA calls this the largest supply disruption in oil market history. Brent at $105 feeds directly into gasoline ($4.15+ per Kalshi at 90%), jet fuel (United slashing guidance), and CPI. Every week Hormuz stays closed adds ~5bp to inflation expectations and increases the probability of a Fed hike — not just a hold.
Reversal signal: Iran agrees to de-mine the strait as part of a peace framework, or UN maritime escort convoys resume. Watch Pakistan-mediated talks for any Hormuz reopening timeline.
US-Iran Ceasefire & Peace Talks
Status: Trump indefinitely extended the ceasefire on April 22. Pakistan mediating. Key issues: freedom of navigation through Hormuz, Iran's nuclear and ballistic programme, reconstruction and sanctions, long-term peace agreement. No face-to-face meetings yet.
Transmission mechanism: Every ceasefire headline sends oil -3% and equities +1%. The indefinite extension removed the binary risk of expiry, but the underlying standoff is unresolved. Equity vol (VIX) is suppressed relative to the tail risk because the ceasefire masks the fact that Hormuz is still closed. The market is pricing "no war" but not "resolution."
Reversal signal: Formal envoy meeting in Pakistan + framework for Hormuz reopening. Without this, the ceasefire is a pause, not a resolution. Any IRGC mine-laying report or US counter-blockade escalation breaks the truce instantly.
FOMC Decision — April 30
Status: 99% hold at 3.50–3.75% (Kalshi). No cut, no hike. But the dissent vote market (86% expect 1 dissent) and the "zero cuts in 2026" market (40%) signal underlying hawkish pressure.
Transmission mechanism: The statement language is the event, not the rate decision. Any shift from "patient" to "prepared to act" on inflation reprices the 2Y yield higher and pressures rate-sensitive equities and crypto. A hawkish dissent validates the "zero cuts" scenario and strengthens the dollar, which is bearish for gold and BTC.
Reversal signal: Dovish statement citing global uncertainty from Hormuz. Unlikely given trimmed mean CPI at 3.5%.
RBA Rate Decision — May 5
Status: 72% probability of a third consecutive 25bp hike. Trimmed mean CPI at 3.5%, well above the 2–3% target. Quarterly CPI data due April 30 could seal or derail the hike.
Transmission mechanism: Another hike pressures ASX 200 REITs and growth stocks, weakens AUD housing, and lifts bank margins. For crypto traders in AUD, rate hikes mean higher AUD-denominated BTC prices but lower real purchasing power. The ASX 200 is already below its 200-day moving average at ~8,800, with support at 8,580.
Reversal signal: April 30 CPI print below 3.3% or a dovish surprise hold. Both are low-probability outcomes given the Hormuz oil shock.
