4 ⚖ Regulatory & Legal Radar

July's regulatory calendar is less acute than feared: ASIC's licensing info sheet was extended to October 29 (not expired June 30), the GENIUS Act implementing rules deadline is July 18 (still ahead), and the CLARITY Act sits on the Senate calendar without an imminent floor vote — the regulatory pressure is real but the near-term market shock risk is lower than yesterday's briefing suggested. The DTCC-Stellar production test timeline for tokenized Russell 1000 / ETF / Treasury settlement in July remains the highest-upside regulatory catalyst for the crypto market — if confirmed, the tokenisation thesis for public blockchains transitions from pilot to production.

Active Legislation

CLARITY Act (Digital Asset Market Clarity Act) — Passed House, Senate General Calendar No. 423. Needs 60-vote floor vote + reconciliation + presidential signature. CFTC as primary regulator for digital commodities (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP) is the structural shift that enables institutional market-making.

GENIUS Act (Stablecoin) — Implementing rules due July 18 from six agencies (Fed, OCC, FDIC, NCUA, CFPB, FinCEN). Reserve requirements, issuer qualification, and AML/CFT standards are the key unresolved items. Non-bank stablecoin issuers face the greatest risk from strict reserve rules.

Today's Decision Points

Australia — ASIC Info Sheet Extended to Oct 29: IS 225, which shields unlicensed crypto platforms from enforcement while they pursue AFSL applications, was extended to October 29 (not expired June 30 as previously understood). This removes the near-term enforcement shock risk for Australian retail crypto platforms — a modestly positive surprise for local crypto liquidity.

GENIUS Act Rules — July 18 Deadline: The detail battle is now in full swing: reserve compositions, cross-chain operational risk, redemption mechanics, and foreign issuer access. Circle (USDC) and Tether (USDT) are the dominant incumbents with different exposure to these requirements.

DTCC + Stellar (XLM) Production Test: July 2026 is the target month for DTCC production testing of tokenized Russell 1000 / ETFs / Treasuries using the Stellar network. Circle's native USDC on Stellar went live June 30. This is the month where the $114T DTCC clearing network makes its first move toward on-chain settlement — watch for an official announcement.

The ASIC extension to October 29 is a quiet positive — it removes the immediate enforcement shock that was priced as a headwind for Australian crypto liquidity. GENIUS Act rules by July 18 remain the key near-term US catalyst. DTCC-Stellar production test confirmation this month is the upside catalyst nobody is talking about but should be. Net regulatory read: less acute near-term risk than yesterday, but July 18 is still a hard deadline that will move stablecoin and RWA tokens.
