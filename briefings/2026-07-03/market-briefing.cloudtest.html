4 ⚖ Regulatory & Legal Radar
EST

The CLARITY Act's window to pass in 2026 is closing fast, and the market has started pricing that in. Polymarket traders now price the bill's 2026 passage odds near 48%, down sharply from 74% a month ago, as the Senate has roughly eight weeks left on its calendar before summer recess and several contentious provisions remain unresolved.

Active Legislation

The Digital Asset Market Clarity Act cleared the Senate Banking Committee 15-9 on 14 May and was placed on the Senate Legislative Calendar under General Orders on 1 June. Democrats Gallego and Alsobrooks, whose votes gave the bill its nominal bipartisan margin, have signaled their committee support was conditional and may not carry over to a floor vote — seven Democratic votes are needed to clear the 60-vote filibuster threshold.

Today's Decision Points

Watch for any scheduling signal on a Senate floor vote before the August recess; analysts view end-of-July as the effective deadline for 2026 passage. A failure to advance before recess would materially deteriorate the bill's prospects and remove one of the few remaining bullish regulatory catalysts crypto markets are pricing in.

The CLARITY Act is now a binary near-term catalyst: a floor vote before recess keeps the regulatory-clarity bull case alive, while a stall pushes the debate into 2027 and removes a prop under institutional crypto allocation.

7 🌎 Geopolitical Calendar

The Strait of Hormuz situation has moved from acute crisis to fragile, monitored normalization — the single biggest swing factor for oil and, by extension, risk sentiment broadly. Two other threads are live enough to matter for the tape today.

🏴 Strait of Hormuz Standoff (Active)
Status: Kpler recorded 34 verified vessel crossings on 30 June, consistent with the 17 June US-Iran MOU holding — but Iran has repeatedly signaled it intends to charge "service fees" for passage once the 60-day toll-free window lapses, and the underlying military standoff from the February air war remains unresolved.
Transmission Mechanism: any breakdown in the MOU immediately spikes WTI and risk-off flows into gold/yen/CHF; continued normalization is why WTI has slid back toward $68.
Reversal Signal: a resumption of IRGC vessel boardings or mine-laying, or Iran formally imposing transit fees, would reprice the risk premium back into oil within hours.
🏴 CLARITY Act Senate Endgame (Active)
Status: ~8 weeks left on the Senate calendar before August recess; conditional Democratic support (Gallego, Alsobrooks) may not convert to floor votes.
Transmission Mechanism: passage odds directly move crypto-equity sentiment (MSTR, COIN, miners) and institutional-allocation expectations for BTC ETFs.
Reversal Signal: a scheduled floor vote date, or public confirmation the bill is being shelved until 2027, would move odds sharply in either direction.
🏴 Fed Leadership Transition Under Warsh (Active)
Status: new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh reiterated on 1 July that "prices are too high," keeping the 10-year pinned near 4.5% and rate-cut odds for 2026 near zero (79.8% probability of zero cuts).
Transmission Mechanism: a hawkish Fed under new leadership keeps real yields elevated, a headwind for both long-duration tech and non-yielding assets like BTC and gold simultaneously — though gold is defying that logic today on safe-haven flows.
Reversal Signal: any dovish pivot in Warsh's language, or a soft CPI/PCE print, would be the catalyst for the 10-year to break back below 4.4%.
Of the three, the Hormuz MOU is the most fragile and highest-impact if it breaks — it is the one geopolitical thread that can reprice oil, gold and equity risk premia within a single session.
