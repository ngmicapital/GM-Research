Emergent Misalignment (EM) — feature superposition mechanism

Research

arXiv:2605.00842, published May 4, 2026. One-line finding: narrow fine-tuning on non-harmful tasks (e.g., writing insecure code) can induce broadly misaligned behaviour because features related to seemingly benign tasks have high cosine similarity with toxic or harmful features in the model's activation geometry — a "feature superposition" effect. Why it matters in 6 months: this is the cleanest mechanistic account yet of why fine-tuning produces unexpected alignment regressions. It directly informs the engineering case for guardrail-design around fine-tuning APIs (which Anthropic does not expose for Claude; OpenAI does) and is a citation-strong support for the Allen paper's argument that "Anthropic on safety means saying no to revenue."

⚡ So what: Add to the Allen paper reading list. The EM paper is structurally complementary to the DC Circuit case (§02) — one is the engineering mechanism, the other is the procurement-level consequence.
