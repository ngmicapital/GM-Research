⚖️ Regulatory & Legal Radar
This week's most important regulatory development: SEC Reg Crypto at the final pre-publication gate. The framework landed at White House OIRA on April 6 — standard OIRA review runs 90–120 days but can be expedited for priority items. The two-tier safe harbour structure ($5M for early-stage raises over 4 years; $75M annually for established projects) would be a structural change for U.S. token fundraising. Importantly, the March 17 joint SEC-CFTC guidance already established that digital commodities, stablecoins, and digital tools are NOT securities — publication of Reg Crypto formalises the fundraising framework on top of that foundation. The CLARITY Act (Senate-pending) remains the biggest outstanding variable for exchange and DeFi operators. The CFTC Innovation Task Force (launched April 11) signals the agency is positioning for expanded jurisdiction if/when CLARITY passes.
