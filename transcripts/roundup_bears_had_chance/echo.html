Bitcoin Bears Had Their Chance

Source TechnicalRoundup ("Casual Friday") — CryptoCred & DonAlt · 18 Sept 2026 · 98 min
Domain Finance / Crypto Markets & Trading Process
Reliability Expert opinion — two veteran discretionary traders' chart reads and self-reported positioning. Every level and headline checked against exchange data and primary sources held up; forward calls are speculative (the hosts' own disclaimer: not financial advice).
Content Type Two-host trading podcast — chart reads and trading-process discussion
Full Mode

⚡ The Bottom Line

  • Bears got their best possible setup and fumbled it. The CLARITY Act failed 49–50 (15 Sept) and the Fed hiked to 3.75–4.00% (16 Sept), yet BTC only wicked to ~74.9K and closed 18 Sept at 80.9K.
  • The hosts' base case has already played out once. Consolidations after an impulse break in the impulse's direction: BTC cleared its 82.75K May high on 21 Sept (~87.3–87.5K). The next real resistance is 93–96K.
  • ETH is the market's confidence gauge — and the strongest major. The 2,350 floor held three times, the daily close above 2.5K came on 18 Sept, and the 2.7–2.8K "first trouble" zone was tagged on 21 Sept (2,809.5).
  • Stops move with structure: the fumbled low is the new line. BTC ~75K warning, ~72K cut; ETH step aside below ~2.3K; SOL invalidated by a monthly close below 100.
  • The process lesson outlasts the calls. Hold two or three strong, liquid names; don't rotate into laggards; avoid cross-margined alt baskets; size to what you can stomach, below Kelly.

🔑 Key Claims (Ranked)

1. The bad-news flush at the range low was the bears' best shot — its failure makes an upside break the base case.
↳ Evidence: CLARITY failed 49–50; the Fed raised 25 bp; BTC's 15 Sept wick reached ~74.9K, then 18 Sept gained 5.9%.
↳ Caveat: Cred commits to reading a failed upside breakout as equally bearish.
↳ Consensus: Confirmed short-term — BTC ran to ~87.3–87.5K on 21 Sept.

2. The monthly never turned; 82K is a speed bump and 93–96K is the real resistance.
↳ Evidence: 82K = the May 2026 high (82,751); 93–96K = the Nov 2025–Jan 2026 range; September printed a green monthly candle.
↳ Caveat: DonAlt scales out as price rises, and admits it usually "still doubles from there".
↳ Consensus: Contrarian to the "80K overbought, lower high" read at the time; vindicated so far.

3. Invalidation belongs at the fumbled extreme, not at a P&L number.
↳ Evidence: DonAlt — below ~75K "would not be great", below ~72K "I definitely cut"; ETH step-aside below ~2.3K.
↳ Caveat: Context-dependent — the 75K line is scrapped only after a push to 90–95K.
↳ Consensus: Aligned with rule-based stops, the standard counter to the disposition effect (Odean 1998).

4. ETH is the strongest major and sets the tone for every altcoin.
↳ Evidence: ETH/BTC made a higher low of 0.0252 (Jun 2026) versus 0.0178 (Apr 2025); ETH closed at 2,613 on 18 Sept and hit 2,809.5 on 21 Sept.
↳ Caveat: By Bulkowski's definition the ETH/BTC "Adam & Eve" only confirms above the ~0.043 peak between the lows.
↳ Consensus: Emerging.

5. Possible is not likely — the bears have been trading hypotheticals.
↳ Evidence: No bear trigger fired in the four-week range; trend-following returns come from trends that survive false starts (Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012).
↳ Consensus: Aligned with trend-following research.

6. Solana's 120–124 should break rather than reject — the Bitcoin-at-6K analog.
↳ Evidence: SOL went from 60.30 (6 Jun) to 120.09 (21 Sept); BTC reclaimed 6K on 9 May 2019, never retested it, and reached ~13.9K by 26 June.
↳ Caveat: Altcoins respect bearish retests more than BTC; invalidation is a monthly close below 100.
↳ Consensus: Pending — SOL tagged 120 on 21 Sept but has not broken it.

7. Real relative strength is walking up while the market is garbage.
↳ Evidence: ZEC rose 32% on 16–17 Sept while BTC was flat; HYPE set an all-time high of $95.99 on 21 Sept.
↳ Caveat: ZEC's record-book ATH is still the $3,191.93 launch-day print (Oct 2016); its September highs are the highest since Nov 2016.

8. Pick strong names and sit — rotating into laggards is where the money is lost.
↳ Evidence: Barber & Odean (2000): the most active fifth of traders earned 11.4% a year against the market's 17.9%; momentum is a priced crypto factor (Liu, Tsyvinski & Wu 2022).
↳ Consensus: Aligned.

9. Size to your psychology, below Kelly, with spot and withdrawals as release valves.
↳ Evidence: Half-Kelly keeps ~75% of the growth at half the volatility, and double Kelly means zero growth; returns to skill shrink with size (Berk & Green 2004).
↳ Consensus: Aligned — fractional Kelly is standard practice.

10. Small accounts have a real, underused edge.
↳ Evidence: Price impact grows with the square root of order size (Tóth et al. 2011), so small traders get cheaper exits and faster entries.
↳ Caveat: The edge fades as the account grows — DonAlt's Salsa Tekila example.
