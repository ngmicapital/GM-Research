⚡ The Bottom Line

  • The one transferable idea is a sizing rule, not a coin call. "Ape like as much size as I can because I hold the floor" 04:02 — buy a book so thin your order slips it 2–3%, capping the loss at "5 to 10%" rather than the 30% air pocket under yesterday's runner 05:04.
  • His two best trades of the week are mid-double-digit gains on large size: "On Banker, I put in like 60, I made 30. On Railgun, I put in 55, I made 65" 06:06. A size-not-multiple book.
  • He round-tripped $200k back to $40k roughly five times in eight months 00:28. The fix is mechanical: "there's no grace in round tripping" 22:11.
  • He is 18, and treats portfolio size as social status on air — "some guys are 30 but they're not valid because they're like five figs" 17:21 — prompting the host's "What am I doing?" 16:54.
