⛓️ Why the Ethereum Version Ran and the Solana Version Died: A Float-Distribution Theory, Not a Chain-Culture One

Like, it still wasn't like 80k or whatever. So, it's not like someone was actually going to get 20% of the supply. Like, someone very well could have had 20% of the supply on the SOL Astroid. 💡 Key Insight

Key Insight — this is a reflexive supply overhang argument, and it generalises far beyond memecoins. The claim is not "Ethereum holders are nicer." It is: the market prices in the exit that the largest early holder is going to take, and it does so before that holder acts. A $500-cost-basis whale sitting dormant for two years is priced as inert; a whale that acquired 15% of the supply nine minutes ago is priced as imminent supply. Same concentration, opposite effect on the ceiling, because the market's estimate of holder behaviour differs. This is the same logic that governs lock-up expiries in equities and unlock cliffs in token launches — and Qwerty derives it from first principles without ever using the vocabulary. 08:15 · 09:26 · 35:20
