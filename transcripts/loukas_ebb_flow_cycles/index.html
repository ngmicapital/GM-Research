Concepts & Frameworks

  • Four-year cycle / timing band — low-to-low intervals of roughly 47–48 months, measured in months rather than to a fixed date
  • Monthly swing — a following candle closing firmly above the high of the downtrend's low candle
  • 10-month moving average as a monthly trend filter
  • Capitulation sequencing — the requirement for a second and third capitulation before front-running a cycle low
  • Cycle low vs price low — a timing low need not coincide with the lowest price
