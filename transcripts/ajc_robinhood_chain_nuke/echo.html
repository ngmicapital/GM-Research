↳ Evidence: leader screened on traction, not chart 02:27–02:31; loss capped in dollars.
↳ Caveat: yields no exit signal 21:08–21:11.

↳ Evidence: "a million dollars of revenue a day in like the DEXs of the bear market" 12:00–12:22, while capital rotated to "institutional" crypto 12:22–12:45.
↳ Caveat: a supply-of-capital thesis, expiring when launchpads become respectable.

3. The buyback multiple is the comparator; PONS was a 5–7x relative discount at entry.
↳ Evidence: Pump.fun 10–15x FDV/buyback vs PONS ~2x, compressing to 1–2x 13:10–13:23 — cheaper as price rose ~60x.

4. Horseshoe theory — launch a token day one or never launch one at all.
↳ Evidence: retail reads absent alignment as intent to extract 14:15–14:28; PONS routes 80% of revenue to buyback-and-burn 14:38.
↳ Caveat: the canonical case did neither — community-launched, then adopted 14:30–14:50.

5. Pair a memecoin to its actual value driver, not the chain's base asset.
↳ Evidence: "ETH ain't going up because AI is going crazy" 19:30–19:53; in an x·y=k pool the coin's value is the pool's 24:20–24:47.
↳ Caveat: the 1:1 link holds "in a vacuum" only 24:52–25:16, breaking on the catalysts you'd buy it for.

6. The peg on a tokenised stock is a discretionary arbitrage switched off outside market hours.
↳ Evidence: mint/redeem is market-hours only 23:33–23:44. Verified: tokenised HIMS printed $61.15 on 30 Aug 2026 against a $28.84 Friday close.
↳ Caveat he omits: the premium converges downward at Monday's open — the weekend holder pays.

Robinhood Chain won on distribution, not technology, and the bull case has a stated expiry — legitimacy by association 08:42–08:50, the forward view conditional on Tenev's unshipped roadmap 07:56–08:10, the revenue memecoins rather than tokenised equities.
