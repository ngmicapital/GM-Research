Is The Bottom In? What The Lads Are Buying

Source Steady Lads Podcast · Ep. 102 · ~84 min
Domain Finance / Crypto Markets
Reliability Mixed — market opinion & self-reported positioning; product/asset claims partially verified against on-chain data & filings; forward calls speculative. Not investment advice.
Content Type Podcast panel / market roundtable
Full Mode

⚡ The Bottom Line

  • A V-shaped recovery ($60k → ~$80–82k, faster than the drop) reset sentiment from Fear-&-Greed ~10 to ~70 in a month. Most of the table thinks the low is in; Justin is the lone holdout — on risk/reward, not direction.
  • The bid is rotated AI/RWA money, not a new crypto-native cohort — except on mobile. FOMO claims the only genuinely net-new audience, onboarding ~40k users/day who "don't realize they're on-chain."
  • Two clean single-name theses dominate: Zcash and Hyperliquid. ZEC on privacy + quantum + "late to Bitcoin, not to Zcash"; HYPE on Trump's White House mention and the pending onshore path.
  • The debasement trade is the macro spine — dollar-trashing → gold + Bitcoin bid — though the "all-time-high BTC–gold correlation" claim is really a ~20-month high.

🔑 Key Claims (Ranked)

1. The bottom is in (~$60k) — high conviction, one dissent.
↳ Evidence: volume and time spent at $60k, capitulation exhaustion ("who's selling again?"), fastest Fear-&-Greed reversal on record.
↳ Caveat: Justin — entering ~$82k for a ~2x to a diminishing-returns top (~$180k?) is weak vs. AI/tech.
↳ Consensus: Aligned with a capitulation-low reading; the upside-math caution is the sharper point.

2. No new crypto natives — incremental money is AI/stock winners rotating back.
↳ Evidence: RWA/perp demand is real, believer demand isn't.
↳ Caveat: FOMO disputes this for mobile; "those guys have a lot of money — if it's just them, that's enough" (Jordi).

3. FOMO: "trade on-chain without knowing it," then IPO — not a token.
↳ Evidence: ~40k new users/day, one balance across all chains, ~90k of ~115k Robinhood-chain wallets, ~0.5% fees, "we're not the house."
↳ Caveat: on-air metrics are ~10× public aggregators (~55k lifetime users) — founder-reported, unaudited.
↳ Consensus: product + ~$75M Series B (Index/USV/Benchmark) verified; the scale numbers are not.

4. Zcash is "the cleanest thesis in crypto."
↳ Evidence: shielded-pool share rising (>$1B pool, >30% of supply, verified), privacy + quantum-resistance, same 21M supply as BTC at ~1% of price → 100x still arithmetically alive.
↳ Caveat: Justin — same "project demand onto the asset" move the industry ran on Bitcoin; buys it anyway.

5. Building in the AI era: taste × agency is the new alpha.
↳ Evidence: the "1000x developer"; AI buffs output (magnitude) for free, so direction (taste) decides; only product-layer and deep-infra builders survive, "everyone in between is obsolete."

6. The "three monies" basket: Bitcoin / private money (ZEC) / an unclaimed AI-money slot.
↳ Evidence: Bittensor named as the failed first AI-money attempt (over-gamified incentives); "most money will be made in AI money."
