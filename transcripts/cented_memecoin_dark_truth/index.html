🧱 Block Zero: Why Buying a Blind New Pair Is a Losing Trade by Construction

"So they have the dev wallet, they sell the dev wallet, and then they have three more wallets in at the floor with like 1.5 soul each… they have all the supply and then there's no risk. They're the first wallets in. So all these platforms have wallets in before anyone can even buy." 19:39–20:05

This is the most concretely useful passage in the interview: a working trader describing, in specifics, why the new-pair layer is not a fair auction.

The mechanic, as he states it:

  • A deployer does a 3–5 SOL dev buy taking roughly 15% of supply 19:27.
  • In the same block, they place two or three additional wallets at ~1.5 SOL each 19:32–19:46.
  • The visible "dev wallet" can then be sold — the optics of a dev exiting — while the hidden sibling wallets retain the real position at floor cost 19:39.
  • Net: "they have all the supply and then there's no risk." 19:51

What changed. He is explicit that this is new: "new pairs used to be everyone would kind of battle it out for first TX… you'd have like a three sol dev. You'd have like a 10% — that was a lot, right? When I first started" 20:13–20:28. The 10% dev that was considered egregious in late 2024 is now the floor, and the race for first TX has been pre-empted entirely.

The consequence for everyone else: "now you have to be so selective on what you're going to buy, especially if you don't have a tracked wallet or a copy trade bot on your wallet… You are just slammed by these block zeros 10 out of 10 times" 20:30–20:42 — unless "you buy something like the next Bitcoin" 20:45.

🌐 Consensus Check His mechanics are accurate and the practice is well-documented. Bundling on Solana means submitting a set of transactions atomically to a block builder (Jito) so they execute in sequence or not at all; a deployer bundles the liquidity-creation transaction together with buys from their own wallets in block 0, before any external sniper can react. Industry writeups are explicit that the presence of a bundle is not itself proof of a rug — legitimate deployers bundle for sniper protection — but that bundle wallets which all sell within ~10 minutes of launch are the rug signature. 🧛 The Vamp — Indefensible, Compulsory, and the Reason Ceilings Fell

Deploy a near-identical clone of a running coin at a fraction of its market cap, pull the flow off the original, and split the narrative's attention in half.

What It Is

A duplicate of a live runner with a marginally "better" ticker, name, or image, launched far lower. The bet is that the market's conditioning to hunt the cheapest expression of a narrative overwhelms the original's head start.

The Practice (as described)

1. Identify a running narrative where the incumbent ticker/name/image is beatable on any dimension 15:04.
2. Deploy or buy the superior duplicate at ~10k while the original sits at ~30k 15:20.
3. Prefer to do it off stream — a tracked wallet buying a vamp at the floor on camera is too visible 09:24.
4. Accept that if you don't, and someone else does, you're "destroyed 10 out of 10 times" on the original 15:58.

Who Does It

Everyone. "It's not streamers vamping, not tracked wallets vamping. It's everyone vamping." 16:09

Cented's Verdict

"I fully agree" it's indefensible 15:33 — and unilaterally stopping is a guaranteed loss.

Second-Order Effect (not stated in the interview)

Every vamp halves the attention available to a narrative. The universal complaint that "coins don't run any more" is the direct arithmetic consequence.

🧱 The Block-Zero Bundle — The Deployer's Riskless Position

Buy your own launch from three or four wallets inside the same block, sell the visible dev wallet for optics, and hold the real position at zero cost basis.

The Mechanic (Step-by-Step)

1. Deploy with a 3–5 SOL dev buy, taking ~15% of supply 19:27.
2. Place 2–3 additional wallets at ~1.5 SOL each in the same block as the dev 19:32–19:46.
3. Sell the dev wallet publicly — the chart shows the dev exiting 19:39.
4. Retain the sibling wallets, which are still in at floor with a dominant share 19:47.
5. Result: "they have all the supply and then there's no risk." 19:51

How to Spot It (Cented's version)

Watch whether the earliest, similarly-sized entries are the ones selling into strength. He doesn't give the rigorous version.

How to Spot It (the rigorous version, from the enrichment)

Trace each early buyer's SOL funding path — if the paths converge on one wallet cluster, it's a bundle. Check wallet age: bundle wallets are typically minted minutes-to-hours before launch with no prior history. Bundle wallets that all sell within ~10 minutes of launch are the rug signature; bundling alone is not, since legitimate deployers bundle for sniper protection.

Consequence

"You are just slammed by these block zeros 10 out of 10 times" unless you have a tracked wallet, a copy-trade bot, or the coin is "the next Bitcoin." 20:35–20:47
