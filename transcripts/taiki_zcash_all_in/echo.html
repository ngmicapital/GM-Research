Zcash: All-in with my Net Worth.

Source Taiki Maeda · 2026-09-11 · 35 min
Domain Crypto Markets & Investing
Reliability Position-holder explainer — Taiki states on camera that Zcash is the asset "worthy of all my attention, all my capital," held spot plus perps. Read as a documented conviction trade, not a neutral survey.
Content Type Investor thesis / trade journal
Full Mode

⚡ The Bottom Line

  • The video is a follow-up recorded eight days after his last one, and its whole premise is that a number printed. ZEC crossed $1,000 on 4 September 2026 for the first time in its ten-year history; Taiki argues the break is structurally significant rather than just a round number, because it drags Zcash above 1% of Bitcoin's market capitalisation and normalises four-digit pricing.
  • His core mechanism is reflexivity, and it is a real mechanism, not a slogan. Zcash privacy is a function of anonymity-set size, so a larger and more valuable shielded pool literally makes the product better. Price and product quality are therefore coupled in a way they are not for a cash-flow asset.
  • The trade is a ratio trade, not a price trade. He is buying the gap between ZEC at roughly 1.6% of Bitcoin's market cap (fully diluted) and a narrative target of 10%, anchored to silver trading at about 12% of gold's market cap.
  • He downgrades Bitcoin inside his own book. "The reason I own Bitcoin is just because it's Bitcoin" — he names Saylor and quantum as headwinds and says he would not be surprised if BTC disappoints the way ETH did last cycle. That is a notable shift from his 3 September framing.
  • The risk disclosure is buried but explicit. "Whenever I'm bullish something, my price targets never get hit… we always stop before." He also expects a "brutal correction" at some point he cannot predict.

🔑 Key Claims (Ranked)

1. Zcash breaking $1,000 is a regime change, not a round number ↳ Evidence: ZEC had never traded above $1,000 in ten years; it broke through with "no resistance" and held, which he reads as holders who bought sub-$1,000 intending to hold for multiples 09:34. ↳ Context: The break happened on 4 September on roughly $34.5m of short liquidations, two weeks after Grayscale converted its nine-year-old Zcash Trust into the first US spot ZEC ETF.

2. Zcash's fundamentals are a function of price, so the asset is structurally reflexive ↳ Evidence: Privacy scales with the anonymity set — $1m in the shielded pool gives a $1m holder nothing; $10bn gives real cover 13:34. ↳ Caveat: The mechanism runs in reverse too; he acknowledges a "brutal correction" is coming and cannot time it. ↳ Context: Roughly 4.89m ZEC — about 28.9% of supply, near $5.8bn — sat shielded in mid-September, against 23.3% a year earlier.

3. The market chose Zcash over Monero on regulatory surface area, not cryptography ↳ Evidence: SEC case closed, a live US ETF, optional and compliance-compatible privacy — so institutions and potentially nation states can hold ZEC but not XMR 14:52. ↳ Caveat: His technical case against Monero — that ring signatures could be unwound by improving AI chain analysis — describes Monero's pre-2026 design; FCMP++ replaced ring signatures in January 2026. ↳ Context: The SEC closed its probe of the Zcash Foundation in January 2026 with no enforcement action, which is adjacent to but not identical to the claim as stated.

4. A "cabal pump" cannot produce this chart ↳ Evidence: Ten years of proof-of-work emissions and a ten-year distribution cannot be cornered the way a new launch can; Elon Musk could not push Doge to new highs even while running a department named after it 16:25. ↳ Context: This is the strongest structural argument in the video and does not depend on liking Zcash.

5. The four-year-cycle bears have pre-announced that they are future buyers ↳ Evidence: He quotes the standard position back — bearish until BTC reclaims $84k, then "happily buying back with max size" — which by construction makes them net buyers either on a Q4 dip or at higher prices 06:08. ↳ Context: Front-running a consensus he does not personally hold is his stated method, not a belief in the cycle itself.

6. Institutional flow is already visible in the tape ↳ Evidence: ETF AUM "over half a billion dollars," plus ZEC repeatedly firming when US equity markets open 17:45. ↳ Context: Correct and datable — Grayscale announced ZCSH passing $500m on 8 September, three days before this recording, alongside the listing of ZCSH options.

7. "Locked in" means the zero-based portfolio test, not degenerate trading ↳ Evidence: If you sold everything today, would you rebuild exactly this book at today's prices? If not, you are misallocated 31:14. ↳ Context: The cleanest transferable idea in the video and the one that does not require any view on Zcash.

8. The optimal bull-market shape is maximum size early, then de-risking into validation ↳ Evidence: "I'm betting really, really big. It feels uncomfortable, but that's what it takes… as the market starts to validate over time, I will slowly take risk off" 33:31. ↳ Caveat: On 3 September he said he had stop losses in place; that control is absent from this recording.
